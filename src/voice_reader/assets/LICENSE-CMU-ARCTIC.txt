The bundled clips are resampled from the original 16 kHz mono PCM16 recordings
to 24 kHz mono PCM16; their speech content is otherwise unedited. The clips
and their exact source URLs and SHA-256 digests are recorded in
builtin_profiles.json. The CMU ARCTIC corpus overview identifies bdl as a US
English male speaker and slt as a US English female speaker:
http://festvox.org/cmu_arctic/

The files use the standard .wave extension for WAV audio so the project's
global *.wav ignore rule does not exclude them from offline distributions.
